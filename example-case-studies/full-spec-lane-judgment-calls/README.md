# Full-Spec Lane Judgment Calls

These case studies calibrate the architectural judgment required before a
responsibility is placed in, retained in, or graduated from the Full-Spec lane
(formerly called the Rebuildable lane). The normative eligibility, charter,
boundary, and verification rules live in the
[Full-Spec Lane guide](../../architecture/module_charters_full_spec_lane.md).

This category is for genuine boundary calibration rather than a presumption for
or against the lane:

- responsibilities that clearly earned Full-Spec placement;
- responsibilities that remained Careful or Critical after concrete comparison;
- ambiguous boundaries that became eligible after ownership was redrawn; and
- disposable comparison-branch workflows that turned abstract architecture
  debates into inspectable evidence.
